title: Salt and Linen
date: 2026-02-14
category: Domestic
tags: home, routine, hands
---
The kitchen wakes before the street,
a kettle humming on low flame,
the table cool beneath our hands.

We fold the clean white linen twice,
measuring width by reach and breath,
saving the seams that hold the edge.

No ceremony marks the salt,
yet here we taste the work of hours,
sweetened by what remains unspoken.
