title: Twelve Seasons of Us
date: 2025-11-04
category: Chronicle
tags: seasons, passage, companionship
featured: yes
---
First came the frost that bit the glass,
etching fern-shapes against the dark,
when we had only just begun.

Then melted ruts along the lane,
the smell of thaw and opened damp,
and boots set drying by the stove.

A sudden green along the hedge,
the swallows cutting through the eaves,
our voices rising with the dawn.

The heat that held the afternoon
in amber stillness, loud with bees,
the shutters drawn against the glare.

Thunder broke across the ridge,
cold rain on sun-warmed stone,
running in rivers toward the ditch.

Then pears that fell before their time,
bruised sweetness on the lawn,
and mornings smelling of woodsmoke.

The harvest loaded on the cart,
the horses breathing white and slow,
our shoulders aching with the sheaves.

Red leaves gathered in the gutter,
the chimney swept, the cellar stocked,
a blanket pulled over the knees.

The first hard gale that stripped the boughs,
leaving the orchard bare and sharp
against an iron-colored sky.

Long nights around the single lamp,
the mending basket on the chair,
no need to fill the empty space.

A silence settling over fields,
snow drifting up against the fence,
the world made simple, white, and whole.

And here we stand once more together,
counting the ring inside the trunk,
grateful for every circle grown.
