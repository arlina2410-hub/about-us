title: The Olive Grove
date: 2026-01-28
category: Nature
tags: earth, quiet, trees
---
Silver leaves turn in the wind,
showing their pale undersides
like secrets kept for fifty summers
along the stony terrace wall.

The soil here drinks without haste,
chalk and clay and brittle grass,
holding the imprint of a boot
long after twilight fills the hollows.

We rested where the trunk had split
into twin branches, curved and gray,
each carrying its share of fruit
toward an unhurried, patient harvest.

To endure without asking why
seems enough in such an orchard,
where even shadows fall gently
across the dry and fragrant earth.
