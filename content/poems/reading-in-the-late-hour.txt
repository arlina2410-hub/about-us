title: Reading in the Late Hour
date: 2026-01-15
category: Memory
tags: books, dusk, solitude
---
The lamp sheds an amber circle
over paper yellowed at the perimeter,
a map of someone else's mind.

Outside, the traffic has thinned
to a single distant murmur,
like water sliding over stones.

A pencil lies in the groove,
its lead worn soft and rounded
from underlining what felt true.

How many evenings ended thus—
a thumb pressed against a corner,
reluctant to close the binding shut?

The room ticks with quiet wood,
settling against the night
as if breathing in its sleep.

We keep these sentences like coins
carried across an old border,
good only in the land we build.
